Business & Group Travel

Move your people with confidence

Practical transport support for teams, guests, families, events, and group journeys.

Travel arrangements can shape the success of a meeting, event, site visit, or family occasion. Vikash Travels helps organisations and groups plan transport that is comfortable, punctual, and easy to coordinate. From a single guest pickup to a multi-vehicle movement plan, we work with you to understand the schedule before assigning the right cars.

For corporate customers, our service can support employee commutes, client meetings, airport transfers, plant visits, intercity travel, and conference movement. A professional driver and a clean vehicle help visitors feel welcomed and allow employees to focus on the work ahead. We can discuss recurring requirements, multiple stops, waiting time, and preferred vehicle categories before confirmation.

For groups, we arrange vehicles for weddings, pilgrimages, family functions, school or college visits, picnics, and local tours. Tell us the number of passengers, luggage needs, route, travel dates, and approximate duration. We can recommend sedans for smaller groups, premium cars for added comfort, or larger vehicles such as Tempo Traveller options when the party needs more seats.

Our approach is straightforward. One contact person shares the plan, our team confirms availability, and drivers receive the relevant pickup and route details. Changes should be communicated early so the schedule can be adjusted responsibly. Final pricing depends on vehicle type, distance, timing, tolls, parking, and waiting requirements.

Vikash Travels is based in Korba and serves routes across Chhattisgarh and beyond. We bring local route knowledge, responsive communication, and a service mindset to every booking. Whether you are organising travel for three people or a full group, contact us for a clear plan and quote.
