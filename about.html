About Vikash Travels

Local knowledge, dependable journeys

Vikash Travels is a Korba-based cab service created for people who want travel to feel simple, safe, and well managed.

Vikash Travels began with a practical idea: every passenger deserves a clean vehicle, a responsible driver, a fair conversation about price, and a journey that starts on time. From our base in Korba, Chhattisgarh, we support families, professionals, visitors, students, patients, and businesses moving between cities and districts.

Our team understands the character of travel in this region. A short local ride can be just as important as a long outstation trip. A hospital appointment, airport connection, wedding, office visit, or family function all depends on dependable timing. We plan each booking around the passenger’s route, luggage, group size, and preferred vehicle so the experience remains comfortable from pickup to drop.

We have built our service around clear communication. Customers can call, message on WhatsApp, or use the booking form to share their travel details. Our drivers are selected for professionalism, local route knowledge, and respectful passenger care. Vehicles are maintained for everyday reliability, with options ranging from efficient sedans to spacious premium cars and group vehicles.

Our mission is client satisfaction, but we measure that in practical ways: reaching the pickup point when promised, sharing useful updates, driving responsibly, and helping passengers feel looked after. As Vikash Travels grows, we want to remain personal and accessible while serving more routes across Chhattisgarh and India.

Whether you are travelling alone or arranging transport for a group, we welcome the opportunity to make your next journey easier.
