Travel Services

A cab for the way you travel

Choose a practical travel service for everyday rides, long routes, airport connections, and group plans.

One-way taxi

Travel from one city to another without paying for an unnecessary return journey. One-way taxi service is useful for relocations, visits, examinations, appointments, and planned outstation trips. Share your pickup, destination, date, and time, and we will help coordinate a suitable vehicle.

Round trips

Keep a trusted car and driver available for the whole journey. Round trips are convenient for family visits, shopping, events, and multi-day travel where you need flexibility at the destination. We can discuss waiting time, route plans, and vehicle requirements before confirming.

Airport transfers

Start or finish your flight journey without last-minute transport stress. Our airport transfer service is planned around your flight schedule, luggage, passenger count, and pickup terminal. We support both airport pickups and drops from Korba and nearby cities.

Local city hire

Book a cab by the hour or for a full day when you have several stops. Local hire works well for meetings, hospitals, shopping, government offices, family functions, and city errands. Your driver can help keep the day organised while you focus on your plans.

Corporate travel

Businesses can use Vikash Travels for employee movement, guest transport, client visits, site travel, and recurring routes. We focus on punctual communication, professional conduct, and practical vehicle selection so your team and visitors can travel confidently.

Group travel

For pilgrimages, picnics, weddings, and family outings, our larger vehicles offer extra seating and luggage space. Tell us the number of passengers, route, duration, and comfort expectations. We will recommend the most suitable option for your group.
